## Research Brief

* What to research: Current Shaper product positioning, implemented capabilities, problem framing, assessment checks, reshaping workflow, visual language, and claim boundaries needed for a Lovable landing-page build brief.
* Why it matters: Lovable needs enough precise direction to create a persuasive page without inventing unsupported functionality or reverting to subjective readiness scoring.
* Audience or intended use: A product or design practitioner will paste the resulting brief into Lovable to generate a public-facing Shaper landing page.
* Scope: README, feature guide, architecture, document-readiness guide, deployment guide, prototype interface, deterministic finding catalog, and directly related tests.
* Non-goals: Implementing the landing page, changing the authenticated product interface, selecting a production hosting platform, or making unsupported commercial claims.
* Criteria: Factually grounded, findings-led, current-versus-planned distinction, complete feature coverage, clear visual and interaction direction, responsive and accessible acceptance criteria.
* Requested outputs: One reusable Lovable build brief in `docs/`.
* Output mode: convergence.

## Research Cycle Log

### Cycle 1

#### Wider wave

Reviewed product positioning, current-MVP capabilities, the Knowledge Estate
workflow, deployment documentation, and the authenticated workspace. The
implemented story is a governed path from enterprise content to findings,
recommendations, approved reshaping, and reviewed outputs.

#### Deeper wave

Verified the 29-check claim as seven baseline checks plus 22 document-quality
checks. Verified current ingestion modes, evidence presentation, version-pinned
approvals, semantic HTML output, before-and-after review, malware scanning,
durable workflow state, and the exact boundary of SharePoint registration.

#### Contrarian wave

Tested likely marketing claims against implementation limits. Rejected live
SharePoint synchronization, automatic estate crawling, autonomous source
rewriting, guaranteed agent accuracy, scheduled governance, distributed workers,
and quantitative readiness scores as current claims.

#### Parent synthesis

One long-form Lovable build brief is the narrowest useful artifact. It should
combine content hierarchy, approved copy points, feature proof, visual direction,
responsive and accessibility requirements, prohibited claims, and a final
acceptance checklist.

## Key Discoveries

* The differentiator is not another retrieval layer. Shaper improves the source
  evidence before an agent consumes it. [C1, C10]
* Findings are more persuasive than a score because each connects a detected
  condition to agent impact and exact evidence. [C1, C6]
* The strongest current feature story is end-to-end governance: ingest, assess,
  recommend, approve, reshape, compare, review, and publish. [C2, C5, C8, C9]
* SharePoint must be described as source registration today, not synchronization
  or crawling. [C3]

## Open Questions, Risks, and Residual Uncertainty

* Final brand assets, customer logos, testimonials, pricing, and production
  contact routes are not present in the repository. The brief must use
  placeholders or omit them.
* No public performance study supports numeric uplift, accuracy, or return-on-
  investment claims.
* Lovable's generated runtime and component library are outside repository
  evidence; the brief should specify outcomes rather than a framework.
